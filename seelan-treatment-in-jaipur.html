Why Jaipur Homes Face Severe Seelan & How PVC Fixes It 100%

By Karan & Jitendra • Updated: Sep 2026

Jaipur is known for its beautiful architecture, but behind the scenes, almost every homeowner is fighting a silent enemy: Wall Dampness (Seelan).

Why Putty and Paint Fail in Jaipur?

Jaipur's soil is highly alkaline (sandy). Over the years, because of changing underground water tables and local drainage issues in heavily populated areas, moisture rises up the walls. This is called capillary action. The water brings salts that crystallize on your wall (Shora), which literally blows the paint off. Repainting every 6 months is just wasting money.

The Permanent Solution: Heavy-Duty PVC Panels

Instead of trying to fight the moisture with paint, smart homeowners are installing 100% Waterproof PVC Wall Panels. Here is why it works perfectly:

  • Air Gap Technology: PVC panels create a 1-inch air gap between the wet wall and the panel. The seelan stays behind, and your room looks 5-star premium.
  • No Fungal Growth: PVC is plastic polymer. It does not rot, catch termites, or grow black mold.
  • 1-Day Installation: No dust, no wet cement. Your room is ready in hours.
