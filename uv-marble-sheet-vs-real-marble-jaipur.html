UV Marble Sheets: The Secret to 5-Star Hotel Walls at Low Cost

By Karan & Jitendra • Updated: Sep 2026

Everyone wants that highly polished, seamless Italian Statuario Marble look behind their TV or in their living room. But real marble is heavy, messy, and extremely expensive to mount on a wall. Enter the ultimate modern solution: UV Marble Sheets.

Real Marble vs. UV Marble Sheets

Mounting real stone on a wall requires breaking the plaster, heavy cement framing, and weeks of labor. The total cost can exceed ₹800 to ₹1500 per sqft.

UV Marble Sheets, on the other hand, are high-gloss, ultra-durable PVC boards coated with UV protection. They look 99% identical to real marble but come with major benefits:

  • Super Affordable: Starting at just ₹120 to ₹150 per sqft (Fully Installed).
  • 1-Day Installation: Pasted directly onto your wall without any cement or dust.
  • Waterproof & Seamless: No grout lines to get dirty. High gloss mirror-like finish.
