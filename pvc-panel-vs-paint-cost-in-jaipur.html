PVC Wall Panel Price vs Putty/Paint Cost in Jaipur

By Karan & Jitendra • Updated: Sep 2026

Many homeowners in Jaipur think that getting a painter to scrape off the damp wall, apply POP/Putty, and repaint is cheaper. But is it really? Let's uncover the true hidden costs.

The "Illusion" of Cheap Paint

A typical painter in Jaipur charges around ₹40 to ₹60 per square foot for dampness repair (scraping, waterproofing chemical, putty, and premium paint). It looks cheap initially. But here is the reality of Jaipur's alkaline water table: The seelan comes back in exactly 6 to 8 months.

So, you are spending ₹50/sqft every year. In 5 years, you have spent ₹250/sqft and your house is always covered in dust from scraping.

The "One-Time" PVC Investment

Premium Heavy-Duty PVC Wall Panels start at just ₹90 to ₹105 per square foot (Material + Labor + Fitting). Here is the magic:

  • Lifespan: Minimum 10 to 15 years guaranteed.
  • Zero Maintenance: It is washable. You never have to paint it.
  • 100% Damp-Proof: The seelan stays hidden behind the panel forever.
